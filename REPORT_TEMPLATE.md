## **Tips for a Great Report:**

✅ **Do:**
- Use tables and charts for clarity
- Include screenshots of test results
- Be specific with numbers
- Explain technical terms
- Proofread for errors
- Use professional language

❌ **Don't:**
- Leave sections empty
- Use vague statements
- Include irrelevant information
- Copy-paste without understanding
- Forget to cite sources

---

## **Grading Criteria (Typical):**

- **Introduction & Background** (10%)
- **Tool Selection & Justification** (20%)
- **Test Cases & Execution** (30%)
- **Results & Analysis** (20%)
- **Recommendations & Conclusion** (10%)
- **Presentation & Documentation** (10%)

---

## **Word Count Guidelines:**

- Executive Summary: 200-300 words
- Introduction: 400-600 words
- Tool Description: 300-500 words each
- Results: 1000-1500 words
- Analysis: 500-700 words
- Conclusion: 300-400 words
- **Total: 3000-5000 words** (typical)
